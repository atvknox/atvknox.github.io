About me

An academically underperforming student from Asia, a GNU/Linux user and an occasional hardware abuser.

I spend most of my free time experimenting with operating systems, configuring Linux environments, writing scripts, and making unsupported hardware do things it probably shouldn't.

Interested in operating systems, system administration, cybersecurity, Android internals, hardware-software interaction (basically, F'ing with hardware from a software's perspective), and open-source software.

When I'm not busy breaking and fixing things, I'm usually watching anime or animated films, listening to EDM, or falling into some other internet rabbit hole.

Systems & Experiments

Arch Linux
My primary Linux installation after getting burned by Gentoo. Btrfs on LUKS2, TPM 2.0-backed disk unlocking with PCR11/PCR15 policies, personally enrolled Secure Boot keys, and a UKI-based boot setup. Running the linux-hardened kernel and nftables with default-drop input and output policies, including explicit Cloudflare WARP and local-network exceptions. Currently running XFCE with Compiz on X11.

Hyprland
Used before switching to XFCE + Compiz. My Hyprland "ecosystem" has its own collection of configurations, scripts and utilities involving Hyprland, Quickshell, Waybar, Rofi, Dunst, Wlogout, Hyprlock, Hypridle, Hyprsunset, and other desktop components.

Learning Lua and rewriting the entire configuration around it was an experience. Writing individual modules was fun until I realised why a small monolithic configuration wasn't such a bad idea after all.

AMD Hackintosh / Ryzentosh
macOS Ventura running on an AMD Ryzen 5 3500U laptop. My first actual experience with Apple software. Thanks to NootedRed, OpenCore and the usual AMD patches, the system is reasonably stable. Almost everything works, except Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. A sarcastic thanks to Realtek, and genuine appreciation for HoRNDIS for making USB tethering possible through my Android phone.

Redmi 7A
Currently running LineageOS 23 (Android 16), rooted with Magisk and running Kali NetHunter in a chroot environment.

I also experimented with Ubuntu Touch on this device. Most things I tested worked, except VoLTE. Couldn't get that working. Definitely a skill issue on my part. Yes, I'm talking about the rotten sandwich: Halium as the kernel, Android firmware blobs, and Ubuntu Touch userspace.

And ofc, who wouldn't have tried EvolutionX and cRdroid? This device probably has seen more operating systems than its working RAM ATP.
